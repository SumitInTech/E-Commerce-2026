# Return & Exchange Policy

## 30-Day Hassle-Free Sizing Guarantee
- If a recommended size does not fit comfortably, customers are eligible for free exchanges within 30 days of delivery.
- Items must be unwashed, unworn, and have original tags attached.
- Footwear and intimate apparel must be returned in their original packaging.

## Fit-Check Verification
- If you used our AI Fit-Check tool prior to purchasing and experienced a sizing discrepancy, your return shipping fee is completely waived, and you receive a 10% credit towards your replacement order.

## Processing Timelines
- Size exchanges are dispatched within 2 business days of return parcel scanning.
- Full refunds to original payment methods take 3 to 5 business days post inspection.
