# Ultra-Creative Luxury E-Commerce Frontend Rules

## Core Principles
1. **Visual WOW Factor**: Every section must exude luxury, fashion prestige, and futuristic cyber-streetwear energy.
2. **Glassmorphism & Neon Sheens**: Use multi-layered frosted glass with subtle borders (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.09)`) and dual-glow shadows (`rgba(0, 240, 255, 0.2)` and `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.15)`).
3. **Dynamic Micro-Interactions**:
   - Floating elements with subtle breathing animation.
   - Interactive hover glows with radial lighting that tracks or highlights upon focus.
   - Laser scanner animations on body scan uploads and AI diagnosis.
4. **Typography Hierarchy**:
   - Syne & Outfit for luxury bold headers and editorial typography.
   - Space Grotesk for biometric telemetry, size specs, and inventory status.
