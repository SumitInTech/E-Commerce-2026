# Fabric Care & Garment Longevity

## Cotton & Linen Care
- Wash in cold or lukewarm water (max 30°C) with mild detergent to prevent shrinkage.
- Line dry in the shade to preserve vibrant colors and prevent fiber breakdown.
- Iron on medium heat while the garment is slightly damp.

## Wool & Cashmere Care
- Hand wash cold or use a specialized delicate wool cycle.
- Do not wring or twist. Dry flat on a clean towel to prevent silhouette distortion.
- Store folded; never hang heavy knitwear as gravity will stretch the shoulder seams.

## Technical Fabrics & Stretch Blends (Polyester / Elastane)
- Avoid fabric softeners as they coat moisture-wicking fibers and reduce elasticity.
- Tumble dry on low heat or hang dry to maintain garment recovery tension.
