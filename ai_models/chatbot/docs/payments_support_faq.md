# Payment Methods, Discounts & Customer Support

## Secure Payment Processing
- **Accepted Payment Gateways:** Credit & Debit Cards (Visa, MasterCard, RuPay), UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm), and NetBanking.
- **Idempotent Transaction Guarantee:** Double-click protection via cryptographic `Idempotency-Key` headers ensures you are never charged twice for the same order.
- **Cash on Delivery (COD):** Available for select pin codes with automated OTP mobile verification upon delivery.

## Returns, Refunds & Sizing Exchanges
- **30-Day Sizing Guarantee:** Free exchanges for 30 days post-delivery if the fit does not meet expectations.
- **AI Fit Waiver:** If you used our AI Fit Lab prior to purchasing and experienced a sizing discrepancy, return shipping is 100% free with an additional 10% wardrobe credit.
- **Refund Processing:** Approved refunds are initiated back to the original payment source within 3 to 5 business days after warehouse inspection.

## Contacting Customer Support
- **Support Tickets:** Submit a dispute ticket directly from the store dashboard under Customer Admin.
- **Escalation SLA:** Priority tickets are resolved by our Bengaluru VIP fashion support desk within 4 business hours.
