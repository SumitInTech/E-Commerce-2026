# Skin Tone, Complexion & Color Harmony Guidelines

## Photometric Skin Tone Analysis & CIELAB Space
- Our AI Color Stylist uses scientific CIE L*a*b* color space and the Individual Typology Angle (ITA°) to evaluate your dermal undertones from a selfie or skin swatch.
- **Fair to Light Complexions (ITA > 40°):** Cool Summer or Soft Spring tones. Look best in muted pastels, optical white, slate blue, powder mist, and charcoal. Avoid washed-out beige or hyper-saturated neons.
- **Medium, Honey & Olive Complexions (ITA 28° to 40°):** Warm Autumn or Warm Spring tones. Look extraordinary in earthy terracotta, burnt orange, golden olive, warm chocolate, mustard yellow, and espresso. Avoid stark chalk-white or neon cyan.
- **Bronze to Deep Complexions (ITA < 28°):** Deep Autumn or Vivid Winter tones. Look magnificent in royal cobalt, pure obsidian onyx, optical white, rich emerald green, burgundy, and warm amber. High contrast silhouettes create a striking visual impact.

## The 4 Seasonal Streetwear Palettes
- **Autumn (Warm & Earthy):** Terracotta, Forest Olive, Warm Bronze, Goldenrod, Espresso, French Flax Linen.
- **Winter (Crisp & High-Contrast):** Pure Onyx, Optical White, Royal Cobalt, Crimson Red, Charcoal Wool.
- **Spring (Luminous & Golden):** Coral Peach, Golden Sand, Warm Cream, Sage Green, Champagne Silk.
- **Summer (Soft & Muted):** Slate Grey, Soft Sage, Powder Blue, Muted Lavender, Vintage Cotton.
