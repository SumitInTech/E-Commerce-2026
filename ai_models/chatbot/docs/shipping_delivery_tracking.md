# Shipping, Express Delivery & Order Tracking

## Delivery Timelines & Carriers
- **Express Air Logistics:** All orders are dispatched via Delhivery Air Express or BlueDart Priority.
- **Metro Cities (Mumbai, Delhi, Bangalore, Hyderabad):** Delivered in 2 to 3 business days.
- **Rest of India:** Delivered in 3 to 5 business days.
- **Free Shipping:** Free express shipping on all prepaid orders exceeding ₹1,499. Flat ₹99 shipping on smaller orders.

## How to Track Your Order
- Every order is assigned an official tracking code (e.g., `ORD-SNITCH-88219` or `TRK-DELHIVERY-9921`).
- You can ask the AI Stylist anytime: *"Where is order ORD-SNITCH-88219?"* or *"Track #88219"*.
- The chatbot performs a live database lookup and returns real-time courier dispatch status, ordered items, and arrival estimates.
- Automated SMS and WhatsApp tracking links are dispatched the moment your parcel leaves our Indiranagar fulfillment hub.
